All About Volleyball

A guide for beginners

Visual Rules

  • There are 2 teams, each of them having 6
  • Front row players usually offend, back row players defend
  • Players would rotate to discover their best position

Service and Receiving

  • When serving, stay behind the end line of the court
  • Your ball must go over the net
  • If the serve lands out and you don’t touch it, your team gets a point
  • You can recieve the ball only 3 times
  • If the ball hits the net during a pass, your team can still make up to 3 hits before sending it over
  • A recieved ball going outside the court lines is out, giving the opposite team a point

Strategies and Techniques

  • Service: Underhand, Overhead, Float, Jump Serve
  • Pass/Recive: Forearm, Overhead, Dig
  • Roles: Setter, Outside/Opposite-hitter, Middle Blocker, Libero
  • Players yell "Mine!" to pass a ball
  • Find open spaces in the oppsite team for the ball to land
  • Bump-Set-Spike Strategy
